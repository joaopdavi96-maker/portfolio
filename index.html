02 — What I do

I think in full funnels. I’m hired for what starts them.

The angle and the ad are where acquisition is won or lost. That’s the work.

Angle & offer research

Systematic competitive research to read what’s scaling and why. I pull apart winning offers, find the mechanism and promise doing the work, and decide where a new angle can win — judgment from years watching these markets, not a swipe file.

Ad creative — hook, script & concept

The ads themselves: hooks that stop cold traffic, scripts and concepts for video and static, and the copy that carries them. Built to be tested, iterated, and scaled — not to win awards.

Advertorial & funnel copy

The bridge between click and checkout: advertorials, sales pages, and the funnel logic that holds attention all the way through. Where most supplement funnels leak — and where I’ve spent most of my career.

Traffic-informed judgment

I’ve run the media, not just handed off files — Meta, Google, and Native (Taboola, MGID). My creative decisions are shaped by what CPA and ROAS actually did, and I speak the same language as the buyers I’d work alongside.
